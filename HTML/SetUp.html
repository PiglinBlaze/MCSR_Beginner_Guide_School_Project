What Is Minecraft

Minecraft is a sandbox survival game where players explore a block-based world, gather resources, craft tools, build structures, and survive against mobs. In speedrunning, these basic skills are used as efficiently as possible.

What Is Minecraft Speedrunning

Minecraft speedrunning means trying to kill the Ender Dragon as quickly as possible. Players collect resources, enter The Nether, obtain Ender Pearls and Blaze rods, locate the Stronghold, and reach The End.
