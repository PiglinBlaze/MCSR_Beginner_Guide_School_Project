The End

Overview

The goal in The End is to kill the Ender Dragon as quickly as possible. Most runs use Beds or Respawn Anchors to deal large amounts of damage. Some advanced strategies, such as zero cycle, may also involve Bows or Crossbows.

One Cycle

One Cycling is a common method for killing the Ender Dragon quickly by using Beds or Respawn Anchors when the dragon perches at the center fountain.

Marcus Fireworks One Cycle Guide

Zero Cycle

Zero Cycle is a more advanced strategy for killing the Ender Dragon before it fully perches. It is not necessary for beginners, but it's good to know about if you want to explore faster and more advanced strategies. Check The Ultimate Minecraft Speedrun Guide by Couriway for more information.
